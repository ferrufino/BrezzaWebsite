How Brezza works

1. Add your route

Import from Strava, Ride with GPS, GPX/FIT or Apple Fitness, or start with the built‑in demo route.

2. Load Vista & AI Prep

See route‑based weather by segment and get an AI gear and nutrition plan tuned to your window, sport, and pace.

3. Check today’s call

Use your readiness band and training summary to decide how hard to go—and save forecasts for quick reuse.
