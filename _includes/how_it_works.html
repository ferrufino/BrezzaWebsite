How Brezza works

1. Add your route

Import Strava, Ride with GPS, GPX/FIT, or Apple Fitness outdoor workouts, or paste a supported URL to drop new routes directly into Vista.

2. Load Vista & AI Prep

Vista forecasts every segment, compares it to the ideal conditions you set per sport, and the AI builds gear + fueling lists tuned to that perfect window.

3. Check today’s call

Your readiness band blends load, sleep, HRV, resting HR, respiration, and SpO₂ so you know whether to push, steady, or dial it back.
