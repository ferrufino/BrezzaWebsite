# Privacy Policy

#### Gustavo Ferrufino built the Brezza app for free to download. The app provides a basic version at no cost and is intended for use 'as is'.

## Notice

This page is used to inform visitors about my policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information for those who choose to use Brezza.

If you choose to use Brezza, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. The Personal Information that I collect is used for providing and improving Brezza. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

Brezza stores limited data to provide sync, sharing, and readiness features. This includes an anonymous app user ID, routes and related metadata (distance, elevation, polyline/altitudes, visibility, origin source), share link records, and optional daily body metrics summaries and baselines derived from Apple Health if you enable HealthKit. We do not sell your data and do not use your data to train AI models.

Strava access tokens remain on-device. Ride with GPS tokens are stored on the backend to enable imports, and you can disconnect at any time.

## Data We Store and Why

- **Routes and metadata**: enable cross-device sync, sharing links, and backup.
- **Share links and view logs**: allow link access and basic share analytics.
- **Body metrics summaries/baselines (optional)**: power readiness and recovery insights if you enable HealthKit.
- **Anonymous app user ID**: links your data without requiring an account.

## Sharing and Public Routes

If you set a route to public or create a share link, the route can be viewed by anyone with the link. You can revoke a share link or make a route private at any time, which disables access to the shared view.

## User Content and Sharing

You own your route data. By using Brezza, you grant Brezza a limited license to store, process, and display your routes solely to provide the app's features (sync, preview, sharing). When you create a share link or set a route to public, anyone with the link can view it. You can revoke or delete shared routes at any time.

## Agreement on the "Import from Strava / Ride with GPS" Features

Importing from Strava or Ride with GPS allows you to bring in Activities, Routes, or Trips you choose. Imported route data is stored in Brezza's backend to enable sync and share links. Strava access tokens stay on your device. Ride with GPS tokens are stored on the backend only to perform imports.

Brezza complies with Strava and Ride with GPS API usage policies. There is no intention of using imported data for AI training.

## Log Data and Analytics

In the event of an error within the app, Brezza may collect data and information (via third-party services) like Firebase Crashlytics. Brezza also uses PostHog for product analytics. This data does not include directly identifying information, but may include device information, app configuration, timestamps, and usage events needed to improve the app.
